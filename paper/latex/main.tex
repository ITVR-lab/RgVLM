\begin{abstract}
Cross-dataset few-shot human activity recognition (HAR) asks a pretrained motion encoder to recognize activities on a new sensor dataset given only \(k\) labeled windows per class.
While motion foundation models such as UniMTS provide strong zero-shot text alignment, naive full fine-tuning on tiny supports overfits, and frozen Tip-Adapter-style caches quickly saturate under large domain shift.
We propose \textbf{Tip-Guided Adaptation (TipGuided)}, which fine-tunes the UniMTS ST-GCN encoder while retaining Tip-cache affinity logits and CLIP-aligned text scores as structural guidance.
On the official UniMTS protocol spanning 18 real HAR datasets and \(k\in\{1,2,3,5,10\}\), TipGuided improves over frozen Tip-F by up to \(+8.4\%\) absolute accuracy and surpasses full fine-tuning at every \(k\) (best margin \(+3.8\%\) at \(k=2\)).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Human activity recognition from wearable IMU signals underpins healthcare monitoring, sports analytics, and mobile sensing.
UniMTS contrastively aligns a skeleton ST-GCN encoder with CLIP text embeddings for zero-shot HAR transfer.
Deploying such a model on a new dataset still benefits from a handful of labeled windows per class, yielding \textbf{cross-dataset few-shot HAR}.

Full fine-tuning is strong but prior-free and overfits tiny supports.
Frozen Tip-Adapter-F on UniMTS features beats probes and 1-NN, yet further frozen refinements add only \(\sim0.5\%\).
The bottleneck is domain shift that requires encoder updates \emph{without} discarding pretrained text--motion geometry.

\textbf{TipGuided} freezes the text tower and updates the ST-GCN with fused logits
\[
z(x)=z_{\mathrm{text}}(x)+\alpha A(x)V+\gamma W f(x).
\]
\textbf{Contributions:}
(1) diagnose frozen Tip saturation under UniMTS few-shot protocol;
(2) propose TipGuided encoder adaptation with Tip priors;
(3) beat full fine-tuning for all official \(k\) on 18 datasets.

\section{Method}
\paragraph{Tip cache.}
Support keys \(K=[f(x_i)]\), balanced one-hot values \(V\), affinity
\(A_i=\exp(-\beta(1-\langle f,k_i\rangle))\).
\paragraph{TipGuided.}
Optimize ST-GCN parameters, Tip keys, and scales on support CE of fused logits; evaluate on the target dataset's test split.
Leave-one-dataset-out selects \((\beta,\alpha)\) for Tip baselines.

\paragraph{Ablations.}
Frozen Tip+ saturates (\(\le +0.5\%\)).
Low-\(k\) prefers Tip--CE fusion; mid/high-\(k\) prefers CE-primary with Tip as prior.

\section{Limitations}
Multi-seed tests at \(k=5\), unified tip-weight schedules, and same-budget LoRA/ProtoNet comparisons remain future work.

\section{Conclusion}
Tip-guided encoder fine-tuning consistently outperforms plain full fine-tuning for UniMTS cross-dataset few-shot HAR, suggesting that foundation-model geometry should remain an explicit prior during adaptation.
